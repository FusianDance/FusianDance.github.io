# Privacy Policy

This website is run by Fusian Dance Crew, a dance group in Munich. Contact for anything about your data: [fusianmunich@gmail.com](mailto:fusianmunich@gmail.com).

We don't use analytics, ads or tracking cookies, and we don't collect personal data through this website ourselves.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open a page, your browser sends GitHub technical data such as your IP address, which GitHub may log for security and operations. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Instagram posts

The home and gallery pages show embedded Instagram posts. To display them, your browser loads content from Instagram (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.), which receives your IP address and may set cookies or link the visit to your Instagram account if you are logged in. See the [Instagram Privacy Policy](https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy).

## Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you.

## Browser storage

The site stores your light/dark theme choice in your browser. This stays on your device.

## Edit mode (crew members only)

Crew members can edit the site after logging in with Google. For this, Google tells us the email address of the account you pick, and we check it against our list of editors. We don't store it. Saved edits are published as commits in our public GitHub repository, attributed to the edit tool, not to you. Unsaved edits stay in your browser until you save or cancel. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data we have about you, and have it corrected or deleted. Write to the address above. You can also complain to a data protection authority, in Bavaria the [BayLDA](https://www.lda.bayern.de).
